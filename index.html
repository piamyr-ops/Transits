How to ride

Bridge the Start and Destination words by entering one directly associated station word at a time.

Example
MOON → NIGHT → OWL → BIRD
  1. You have 15 seconds for each transfer. Incorrect words do not reset your timer.
  2. Use meaningful links: a shared meaning, object, use, material, or double meaning. The referee checks built-in associations, imported human/lexical data, Datamuse, ConceptNet, dictionary definitions, Wikidata and the optional on-device semantic model. The clock pauses briefly for submitted answers and train travel; offline play uses the local graph. Specific shared associations and contextual connections can also count. Unrecognized links may be rejected, even if valid.
  3. Each valid transfer earns a base score tier: Local Train, Express Train, Limited Express, First Class, or Luxury Train. Final score = (100 + 10 × average points per stop) × 8 × 0.5^(stops − 1). Every added stop halves your multiplier; even an all-Luxury route cannot beat a shorter completed route. Assists and misses count in the average. Route grades compare against a fixed reference route.
  4. Take the Local uses a common available association for zero base points and counts as one stop. It may help you advance. Time running out adds a missed-train detour that never gets you closer. If no such move exists, the detour counts as a stop but you stay put.
